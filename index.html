2 · Trust

People only book from someone they trust.

Every design is built on the same trust checklist. Guests should be able to answer "is this real, is it good, is it safe to book?" without scrolling.

  1. Real photography above the fold — your building, your rooms, your plates.
  2. One clear promise in the headline — what it is, where it is, why it's worth it.
  3. Booking button always visible — in the header, on every page, on phones too.
  4. Reviews near the top — real quotes with the source named.
  5. Open-now status and hours — no hunting for "are you open tonight?"
  6. Prices shown with tax — no surprises at the last step.
  7. Real map and address — a pin in German Village, not a stock photo.
  8. Readable and accessible — high-contrast text, large tap targets, keyboard friendly.
  9. No pop-ups on arrival — nothing between the guest and the room.
  10. Fast on a phone — where most guests will first see it.
