What every option already does

  • Book a room: dates → room (live availability and prices incl. tax) → guest details → review → confirmation code and calendar file
  • Reserve a table: party size, date and available time slots (closed Mondays) → details → confirmation
  • Manage booking: look up by code and last name, view details, cancel with confirmation
  • Menu: breakfast, lunch, dinner and drinks, vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free filters, search — hover (or tap) a dish to see its photo
  • Rooms: filter by guests and bed type, sort by price or size, detail pages with photo gallery
  • Contact form with event enquiries, FAQ, hours, a real map of German Village, newsletter sign-up, and phone-friendly menus

Placeholder name, address, phone and reviews are marked in src/content.js, ready for your consultant's real content. Photos are AI-generated stand-ins and will be replaced with your own.
